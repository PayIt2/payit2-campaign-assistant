# Connecting PayIt2 in this chat

Use this wording when a skill needs the organizer to connect PayIt2. Keep it to a few lines.

**In ChatGPT:**

> 1. When ChatGPT asks to connect PayIt2, choose **Connect**. If it does not ask, open the PayIt2 plugin from your apps and choose **Connect**.
> 2. On the PayIt2 sign-in page, choose **Continue with Google** or **Email me a sign-in link**. If you are new to PayIt2, either one creates your free account in the same step.
>
> Then tell me "connected".

If the organizer says "connected" but the PayIt2 tools still are not available, ask them to start a new chat and run the command again. Do not loop on this.
